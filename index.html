Kuhn App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Kuhn app ("the app"), published by Kuhn Seramik, handles your information.

Photos

When you use the visualization or AI redesign features, the photo you choose is sent to Google's AI service (Gemini, via Firebase) only to create the image you asked for. We do not keep your photos on any server. Created designs are saved only on your phone, and you decide whether to save them to your gallery or share them.

Purchases

Design packs are sold through Google Play and the App Store. Payments are handled by them; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase records, not your name or email.

App security

The app uses Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest) to make sure requests come from the genuine app. This uses device signals and is not used to identify you.

Quote requests

If you request a quote, the app opens WhatsApp or your email app with a prepared message. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

What we don't do

No account or sign-in is needed. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Data on your phone

Your design archive and remaining design count are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
